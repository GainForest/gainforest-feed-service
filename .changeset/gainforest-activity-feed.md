Turn the service into the GainForest activity feed over Hyperindex. The Hypercerts feed, its `org.hypercerts.feed.*` procedures and Lexicons, the hydrated Hypercerts views, and every HappyView dependency are removed.

The service serves `app.gainforest.feed.getFeed` and `app.gainforest.feed.getFeedSkeleton` for the feed `app.gainforest.feed.defs#activityFeed`. `getFeed` returns references and selection metadata, not records: AT-URIs, CIDs, account DIDs, timestamps, and group counts. GainForest hydrates the referenced records itself. The only param is `filter` (`all`, `project`, `observation`, `organization`, `post`, or `audio`), and `params` is optional: omitting it selects the global feed. The feed is the same for every caller: authentication is optional, a service-auth token that is supplied is verified (an invalid one is HTTP 401), and nothing in the response depends on it. A property the Lexicon does not define is ignored. Reposts count only while their subject is a valid observation, project, or top-level post by another account.

Items are `recordItem` (a project, observation, organization, post, or audio recording), `repostItem` (both omit `cid` when Hyperindex has not stored one), and `groupItem`. A `groupItem` is a card of at least 3 observations by one account, or recordings in one recorder folder: `kind`, `did`, `deployment` (audio only), `groupKey`, `total`, `from`, `to`, up to 20 `subjects`, and `more`. A recording's folder is owned by the account in its AT-URI when that is a valid DID; a recording without a folder, or whose folder names a handle or anything else that is not a valid DID, belongs to its own account. `deployment` is omitted when the recordings' `deploymentRef` is not a valid AT-URI, so one malformed value cannot fail a page.

Grouping does not depend on the rows around a group. A row joins its group's open card when it is no more than 12 hours older than that card's oldest row, whatever other rows lie between them, and a larger gap starts a new card. A card sits at its newest row. A group of fewer than 3 rows is returned as separate `recordItem`s, each at its own position. Bioacoustic sightings, reposts, posts, projects, and organizations never group. So two recorders that upload at the same time give one card each.

`limit` counts cards, from 1 to 50, so a page holds between `limit` and `2 × limit` items, and fewer when the end of the data or the row budget ends it. A request consumes at most 5,000 rows, in statements of 500 rows, so it runs one statement for most pages and at most 10. A page that reaches the 5,000 rows always has a cursor, and sets `more: true` on a group card that a later row can still join. `groupKey` is a stable hash of the group's kind, owner, and folder: the same on every page and in every request. A group that continues on a later page comes back as another card with the same `groupKey`. Clients merge it into the last card shown for that key when its `to` is no more than 12 hours before that card's `from`: add `total`, take the later `from` and `more`, and append `subjects`. The absence of `more` does not mean that a group is complete.

`getFeedSkeleton` flattens the same pages to AT-URIs in item order; a group yields every row it covers, newest first, so a skeleton page can hold up to 5,000 entries. The cursor is version 3: unpadded base64url JSON that holds the last consumed row and nothing else. A cursor of another version, or whose timestamp is a valid datetime that PostgreSQL cannot read (a day the month does not have such as `2026-02-30`, year 0000, or a UTC offset beyond ±15:59), is rejected with `InvalidCursor` (HTTP 400); clients request the first page again.

The service reads Hyperindex's `record` table. Before deploying, run Hyperindex migrations 010 and 012, give the service's role `SELECT` on `record`, and point `DATABASE_URL` at the Hyperindex database. `GET /ready` checks that the role can read the columns the feed uses. `TRUSTED_QUALITY_LABELER_DIDS` is ignored. Feeds order by Hyperindex's `record_created_at` and include only records Hyperindex has validated, dated from 1970 up to five minutes ahead. The feed statement is a plain read of the next 500 rows after the cursor. The database pool opens every session with `jit=off`. No migration or index change is needed beyond migrations 010 and 012. The DID document service entry is `#gainforest_feed` (`GainForestFeedService`). Index proposals for the Hyperindex owners are in `docs/database-contract.md`.
