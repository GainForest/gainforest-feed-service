Turn the service into the GainForest activity feed over Hyperindex. The Hypercerts feed, its `org.hypercerts.feed.*` procedures and Lexicons, the hydrated Hypercerts views, and every HappyView dependency are removed.

The service now serves `app.gainforest.feed.getFeed` and `app.gainforest.feed.getFeedSkeleton` for the feed `app.gainforest.feed.defs#activityFeed`. `getFeed` returns references and selection metadata, not records: AT-URIs, CIDs, account DIDs, timestamps, and group counts. GainForest hydrates the referenced records itself. Items are `recordItem` (project, observation, organization, or post), `repostItem` (both omit `cid` when Hyperindex has not stored one), `burstItem` (one or more rows of a run of adjacent observations by one account with no gap over 12 hours, with a total and up to 20 sample URIs; a lone observation is a `recordItem`), and `audioItem` (a run of adjacent recordings in one recorder folder, whoever stored them, owned by the account in the folder's AT-URI; a recording without a folder is grouped by its own account). `getFeedSkeleton` flattens the same page to AT-URIs. The params are `filter` (`all`, `project`, `observation`, `organization`, `post`, or `audio`), `following`, and `viewerDid`. `params` is optional: omitting it selects the global feed, and the anonymous global feed needs no viewer. Reposts count only while their subject is a valid observation, project, or top-level post by another account.

The service reads Hyperindex's `record` table. Before deploying, run Hyperindex migrations 010 and 012, give the service's role `SELECT` on `record`, and point `DATABASE_URL` at the Hyperindex database. `GET /ready` now checks that the role can read the columns the feed uses. `TRUSTED_QUALITY_LABELER_DIDS` is no longer read. Feeds order by Hyperindex's `record_created_at` and include only records Hyperindex has validated, dated from 1970 up to five minutes ahead. The DID document service entry is now `#gainforest_feed` (`GainForestFeedService`). Index proposals for the Hyperindex owners are in `docs/database-contract.md`.
